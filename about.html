about me

Hello, my name is Michelle, and I’m a creative, storyteller, and designer.

I’m a recent graduate from Northwestern University, having studied Journalism, Asian Languages and Cultures, and Design. I love to create: from leading the direction of magazines to managing cohesive social media content, I’ve found ways to blend my love for visual design and storytelling.

With a background in journalism and design, I want to take my skills into creating new kinds of content within entertainment. 

My goal is to fill my cup with the things that fuel my creativity at every hour of the day—hence the slogan, “It’s Michelle ‘o clock!”
